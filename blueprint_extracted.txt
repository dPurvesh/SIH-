The platform must provide evidence-linked findings and a clear verification status while retaining regulatory scrutiny, inspection and final decision-making with the competent AICTE authorities. 1.1 Detailed Analysis of the Problem in Hand (Current Bottlenecks) Every academic year, the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) processes thousands of institutional applications for Extension of Approval (EoA), establishment of new institutions, increase in intake, introduction of new courses (including BBA/BCA/BMS), and institutional status changes in accordance with the AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH). Currently, the scrutiny and verification lifecycle suffers from five critical operational bottlenecks: 1. Massive Document Volume & Repetitive Manual Checking:  A single institutional application contains 30 to 60+ multi-page documents—including Affidavits, Land Registration Deeds, Municipal Building Plans, Fire Safety NOCs, Lease Agreements, Bank Statements, TER Payment Receipts, and NAAC/NBA Accreditation Certificates. Manual scrutiny by Regional and Bureau officials takes 3–4 hours per file, causing severe processing backlogs. 2. Cross-Document & Historical Data Inconsistencies:  Crucial attributes such as Plot Survey Numbers, Total Built-up Area (sq. m.), Approved Intake, and Society/Trust Registration details are scattered across multiple uploaded PDFs and legacy AICTE databases. Detecting subtle contradictions across a 50-page Land Deed and an Architect Floor Plan is nearly impossible through manual visual scanning. 3. Vulnerability to Document Manipulation & Forgery:  Unscrupulous applicants may digitally alter expiry dates on expired Fire Safety NOCs or Building Stability Certificates, forge authorized signatory stamps using image editors (Photoshop/Canva), or upload recycled documents from previous years. 4. Fragmented Issuing-Authority Verification:  Confirming whether a submitted certificate (PAN, Trust Deed, Fire NOC, Occupancy Certificate, Digital Signature) is genuine requires manual follow-up or visiting disparate external portals, leading to verification delays and unverified approvals. 5. Unverified Physical Infrastructure Claims:  Institutions submit photographs, videos, and floor plans to prove adequate computer labs, classrooms, library resources, and barrier-free facilities (ramps/lifts). Without automated Computer Vision checks, duplicate photographs (reusing the same computer lab photo for multiple departments or across different colleges) and shortfall in equipment count go unnoticed until physical EVC inspection. 02   CORE OBJECTIVES (MAPPED TO AICTE REQUIREMENTS) # Official AICTE Objective (ID26261) How Our Solution (PRAMAAN-AICTE) Achieves It O1 Identify missing, incomplete, expired or inconsistent information and documents at the application stage. It operates as a 5-Pillar Validation & Scrutiny Engine: Pillar 1: Intelligent Application & Cross-Document Validation Engine Category-Driven Dynamic Checklists:  Upon selecting the application category (e.g., Extension of Approval, New Institution, Increase in Intake, ToC, Closure), the system loads the exact mandatory checklist from a configurable JSON/YAML APH Rule Engine. Multimodal OCR & Structured Extraction:  Uses Surya OCR / PaddleOCR paired with schema-constrained LLM extraction (Pydantic v2) to extract structured entities (Survey Numbers, Built-up Area, Expiry Dates, Issuing Authority, Trust Name, Intake Numbers) from noisy, multi-column scanned PDFs and bilingual government certificates. Cross-Document Entity Graph Reconciliation:  Constructs an application-level Entity Graph that cross-verifies extracted values across multiple documents and historical AICTE records (e.g., matching Plot Survey No. and Area across the Land Registration Deed, Municipal Approved Building Plan, Fire Safety NOC, and Form Part-1). Pre-Submission Deficiency Rectification:  Generates an instant, structured Preliminary Validation Report allowing the institution to rectify missing fields or expired uploads before entering the official AICTE scrutiny queue. Pillar 2: Document Forensics & Potential Forgery Detection Engine Error Level Analysis (ELA) & Pixel Forensics:  Analyses JPEG/image compression residuals using OpenCV and SciPy to detect localized pixel manipulation around expiry dates, numerical figures, and official stamps, generating an interactive visual heatmap overlay. PDF Structural Metadata & Font Tree Inspection:  Inspects internal PDF dictionaries (/Producer, /Creator, /ModDate, XRef tables, and embedded font subsets) via PyMuPDF to detect files modified in consumer editing tools (Photoshop, Canva, online PDF editors) or mismatched font encodings on altered lines. Perceptual Hashing (pHash) & Cross-Application De-duplication:  Computes cryptographic (SHA-256) and perceptual hashes (pHash) alongside vector embeddings to detect if an affidavit, NOC, or certificate has been duplicated within the same application or copied from another institution's submission. Advisory Safeguard:  Every forensic alert is strictly labelled as a 'Preliminary Indicator Requiring Human Verification' with its underlying evidence attached. Pillar 3: Issuing-Authority Verification Gateway Multi-Channel Source Authentication:  Verifies documents via four automated mechanisms: (1) X.509 PKI Digital Signature (DSC) cryptographic validation using pyHanko, (2) High-density QR Code & Barcode payload decoding, (3) Unique Reference Number (URN) lookup via API Setu / DigiLocker / NSDL / NAAC / NBA / State Fire & Municipal Mock Sandboxes, and (4) TER Payment & FDR status verification. Six-State Deterministic Status Classification:  Stamps each document with source name, query timestamp, response hash, and one of the six mandated AICTE statuses: VERIFIED, MISMATCH_DETECTED, EXPIRED, VERIFICATION_PENDING, SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE, or MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Pillar 4: Regulatory & Infrastructure Computer Vision (CV) Auditor Automated Equipment & Capacity Verification:  Applies fine-tuned YOLOv8 object detection on submitted computer lab, workshop, and classroom photographs to count computer terminals, desks, and fire extinguishers, comparing them against APH norms for the requested student intake. Barrier-Free & Facility Verification:  Detects physical accessibility compliance indicators (ramps, tactile pathways, lifts, washrooms) and library infrastructure. CLIP Visual De-duplication & EXIF Geo-Fencing:  Generates OpenAI CLIP visual embeddings stored in pgvector to immediately flag if two labs (or two different colleges) submit photos of the exact same room from different angles. Simultaneously validates EXIF GPS coordinates and capture timestamps against the institution's registered campus polygon via the Companion Mobile PWA. Floor Plan OCR & Area Cross-Check:  Extracts room dimensions and carpet area tables from uploaded Architect Floor Plans and compares them against AICTE APH norms (e.g., 66 sq.m per classroom, 66 sq.m per lab). Pillar 5: Centralized Validation Dashboard, Alerts & Immutable Audit Trail Split-Screen Official Scrutiny Workbench:  Provides Regional Officers, Scrutiny/Re-Scrutiny Committees, and EVC members with a side-by-side interface: interactive document viewer with OCR bounding boxes and ELA heatmaps on the left, and APH clause checklists, source-verification badges, and override controls on the right. WORM Storage & Hash-Chained Audit Ledger:  Stores all uploaded evidence in MinIO with Object Locking (Write-Once-Read-Many) and records every rule applied, API payload, alert, and official decision in a SHA-256 hash-chained PostgreSQL audit table. 04   INNOVATION & UNIQUENESS (COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATORS) Unlike generic wrapper solutions that merely pass text to an LLM, PRAMAAN-AICTE introduces six deep-tech engineering innovations tailored specifically for regulatory governance: 1. Cross-Document Entity Consistency Graph:  Instead of validating documents in isolation, our engine builds a unified entity graph per application—automatically reconciling Land Area, Survey Numbers, Trust Names, and Intake capacities across 10+ distinct legal and architectural documents. 2. Explainable Visual Forensics (ELA + Font Subsetting):  Combines pixel-level Error Level Analysis (ELA) compression heatmaps with PDF XRef/Font-tree structural inspection to pinpoint exact tampered regions (such as an altered NOC expiry year) with visual proof rather than opaque black-box scores. 3. Semantic Visual De-duplication via CLIP Embeddings:  Standard MD5/SHA hashes fail if an institution crops, rotates, or slightly re-shoots a lab photograph. By indexing every submitted infrastructure photo in pgvector using CLIP embeddings, our system catches visually identical rooms across departments, academic years, and even across different institutions nationwide. 4. Live Geo-Fenced Capture Companion PWA:  Eliminates gallery-uploaded fake photos by providing a lightweight Mobile Companion PWA for Nodal Officers and EVC Inspectors that locks camera capture to live hardware GPS coordinates, compass azimuth, and network time. 5. Zero-Code Configurable APH Rule Engine:  Decouples AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) norms from application code using a dynamic JSON/YAML rule schema + RAG policy assistant. Whenever AICTE releases a new APH addendum or revises Student-Faculty / Land ratios, administrators can update thresholds via UI without redeploying code. 6. Court-Admissible Hash-Chained Audit Trail & WORM Storage:  Every AI observation and human override is cryptographically chained (each log row hashes the previous row's signature) alongside MinIO WORM storage, guaranteeing 100% non-repudiation for statutory RTI or legal scrutiny. 05   END-TO-END SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & EXECUTION WORKFLOW 5.1 Six-Stage Execution Workflow Stage Workflow Phase Technical Operations Executed Stage 1 Category Selection & Dynamic Checklist Load Institution logs in via AICTE Permanent ID -> Selects APH Category (EoA / New / Intake Increase) -> Rule Engine fetches historical AICTE records & renders mandatory document/infrastructure checklist.
